# Code of Conduct

AlphaLens welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, specific and constructive in issues, pull requests and other project discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims and code, not people. Explain disagreements with evidence.
- Be patient with questions; make room for different perspectives.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property and data-use restrictions.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, and investment promotion unrelated to the project are not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

The maintainer, [axlezhao](https://github.com/axlezhao), may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Use GitHub's reporting/blocking tools for abuse or contact the maintainer through a private contact method they list on their profile. Do not post private information in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities have a separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to project spaces and to individuals representing this project. It does not create a support or response-time guarantee.
